## 🌿 Logo

The logo consists of:
1. **Icon:** A leaf/seed shape with organic vein/root patterns inside — representing the intersection of nature (organic growth) and technology (network/code structure)
2. **Text:** "ROOT CODE" in clean, dark green sans-serif below the icon
3. **3D Brand Object:** A geometric cube/block with a leaf pattern carved into one face — represents the physical manifestation of the brand identity

### Logo Colors:
- Icon fill: Primary (#1B3022)
- Vein patterns: Secondary (#98A99A)
- Text: Primary (#1B3022) on light backgrounds

## 🧘 Visual Philosophy: "O Equilíbrio Orgânico" (The Organic Balance)

The visual identity is guided by "Eficiência Silenciosa" (Silent Efficiency). Unlike traditional tech brands that use aggressive contrasts and overloaded elements, this approach focuses on the essential and harmony.

### 1. Minimalismo Estrutural (Structural Minimalism)
- Absolute White is functional, not just aesthetic
- It serves as "air" that lets logic breathe
- Every visual element must have a clear purpose
- Eliminate any noise that doesn't contribute to service understanding

### 2. Geometria Suave (Soft Geometry)
- Software is built on logic and grids, but the visual representation avoids sharp edges
- Slightly rounded borders and forms resembling river-polished stones
- Solid, but gentle to the touch

### 3. Cromatismo Terroso (Earthy Chromatism)
- Forest Green dominance: authority of deep roots
- Sage accents: lightness of innovation without aggression
- Typography contrast: white on dark or dark gray on light for absolute clarity

### 4. Ritmo e Espaçamento (Rhythm & Spacing)
- The brand must breathe
- Generous margins, generous line heights, few elements at a time
- Transposing the sensation of a zen garden into a code interface

### 5. Simbolismo das Raízes (Root Symbolism)
- Iconography always references growth structures
- If code is the base, it grows like a root: firm, invisible, yet sustaining the entire upper structure
